    about

    Dave Hawkins writes songs meant for slowing down in a fast-moving world. Based in Denver, operating out of a home studio, he blends classical piano with synth textures and indie guitar rock.

    His new EP Half Alive looks at the loops we get stuck in and the moments that break us out.
